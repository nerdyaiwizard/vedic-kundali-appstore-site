Privacy Policy

Vedic Kundali calculates Vedic charts from user-provided birth and location details.

Data collected

  • Birth date, birth time, timezone, and optional fold
  • Place label, latitude, longitude, and optional elevation
  • Optional requested calculation time (`asOf`)

How data is used

The app sends this information to the calculation backend over HTTPS to generate the chart response.

Storage

The iOS app may store recent charts on the device so you can reopen them. The backend is intended to operate without retaining user chart histories.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs in v1.
