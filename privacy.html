Privacy Policy

How Vedic Kundli handles chart inputs.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Vedic Kundli sends the birth details you enter to its calculation service so it can return the chart you requested. That service does not keep a chart account or a chart history. Recent charts are stored on your device, and you can delete them in Settings.

Data used for calculation

  • Birth date
  • Birth time
  • Timezone
  • Place label
  • Latitude and longitude of the birth place you select
  • Optional elevation and reference time

The app does not ask for your current location, contacts, photos, or tracking permission.

Storage

Recent charts are stored only on this device and can be cleared from Settings. Chart requests are not written to an application database. The service may keep an IP address in memory for a short rate-limit window. It does not build an advertising profile from that address.

Purchases

The optional annual subscription and the lifetime pass are processed by Apple. Vedic Kundli does not receive your card number. You can restore either purchase from the purchase screen or from Settings.

Sharing and tracking

The app does not sell data, show ads, or use tracking SDKs. No account or login is offered, so Apple's in-app account deletion requirement does not apply. Readings are for personal reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, financial, or professional advice.
