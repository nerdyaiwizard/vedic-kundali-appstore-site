How Vedic Kundli handles chart inputs.

Last updated: June 17, 2026

The app sends birth details, timezone, place label, and coordinates to the calculation backend to generate the requested chart. The backend does not store chart request history in the application database. Recent charts are stored locally on the user's device and can be cleared from Settings.

Data used for calculation

  • Birth date
  • Birth time
  • Timezone
  • Place label
  • Latitude and longitude
  • Optional elevation and reference time

Storage

Recent charts are stored locally on the user's device and can be cleared from Settings.

Sharing and tracking

No ads or tracking SDKs are used in v1. No account creation or login is supported, so Apple's in-app account deletion requirement is not applicable.
