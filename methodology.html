Methodology

How the chart is calculated.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Vedic Kundli uses a hosted Python backend with deterministic astronomy and Vedic chart settings. The app is designed to make the calculation path traceable instead of opaque.

Calculation stack

  • Skyfield with the JPL DE440 ephemeris for apparent geocentric planetary positions. Elevation is stored with the place but is not applied to the positions or the ascendant.
  • PyERFA for Earth orientation and precession-related formulas
  • Python zoneinfo for timezone and DST rules. Birth time is civil wall-clock time in the IANA zone, not local mean time.
  • ICRC Lahiri ayanamsa: 23.853222 degrees at J2000 plus IAU 2006 general precession in longitude. This zero point is about 14 arcseconds from the Swiss Ephemeris Lahiri value.
  • Mean lunar node with Ketu exactly opposite Rahu
  • Whole-sign houses. The ascendant is the ecliptic point rising on the eastern horizon, including latitudes where the raw trigonometric result is the descendant.
  • D9 Navamsha from the sidereal longitude, using the standard movable, fixed, and dual sign starts
  • Vimshottari mahadasha and antardasha. Yogas, doshas, and panchang are not calculated.

Input rules

  • Birth dates must use DD/MM/YYYY
  • Birth time is a local wall-clock time
  • An IANA timezone is required
  • Backward DST transitions use the fold value
  • Different calculators may disagree if they use a different ayanamsa, node model, house model, or timezone source

Interpretation limits

The app provides educational and cultural interpretation only. It does not make guaranteed claims about health, money, law, relationships, or destiny.

Results remain sensitive to exact birth time, timezone accuracy, and coordinates.
